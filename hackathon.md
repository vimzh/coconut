## Eligibility and integrity rules

- Enter individually; one submission per participant. Accept the Participant Agreement before submitting. [Event][event]
- Be at least 18 at registration. The agreement additionally requires **two or more years of experience in any field**, plus coding or AI coding skills. [FAQ][faq], [Agreement][agreement]
- Residents of Afghanistan, Belarus, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Iran, Iraq, Libya, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, and Yemen are excluded. Core staff, administrators, judges, and challenge creators/testers cannot win prizes. [FAQ][faq]
- Submit original work without confidential employer/client data; cheating disqualifies an entry. [Event][event]

## Required submission

Submit through HackerRank:

1. **One ZIP, maximum 50 MB:** eval code and README. The guide permits small supporting files; exclude videos and credentials. [Event][event], [Guide][guide]
2. **A publicly accessible video link, maximum five minutes:** walkthrough of the eval. [FAQ][faq]
3. **A short written description:** what you built and the model limitation it reveals. [Event][event]

## Guide checklist and judging expectations

- Make tasks unambiguous; state everything the grader checks.
- Supply verified reference solutions that pass, and deliberately wrong answers that fail.
- Test at least one frontier model with multiple trials per task; explain failures from transcripts.
- Include representative transcripts for every reported model and at least one failure per task.
- Document capability, practical stakes, task difficulty, grading validation, run commands, dependencies/versions, environment variables, model settings, trial counts, score spread, cost/runtime, data licenses, prior work, AI use/review, and the main finding.
- Keep reference answers hidden from tested models. Review every task, answer, and grading rule yourself.
- Use only shareable, licensed data; exclude private personal information.

These are the participant guide's preparation and judging expectations. [Guide][guide]

### Recommendations

Aim for **5–10 tasks**; there is no maximum or required failure rate. [FAQ][faq] Start with **3–5 trials per task**. Prefer code-based graders where suitable; Harbor is optional. [Guide][guide]
