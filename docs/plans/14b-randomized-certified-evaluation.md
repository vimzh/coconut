## Finite work and admission gate

- Requested source depth: 1–1000; realized source depth must be D or D+1. Proposed maximums: 16,384 parsed nodes, 4,096 parse depth, 131,072 expression characters, 16 MiB CSV field, 64 MiB source file and 120 seconds per external generation batch.
- At most 24 received candidates per panel in deterministic class/ordinal order; stop explicitly if any class cannot be admitted. Record upstream internal sampling behavior separately from received-candidate rejection counts.
- Independent proof work is bounded per candidate and per process. The exact supported operation/domain policy and tested bounds must be recorded after feasibility inspection; unknown is never a label. Neither needs rigorous strict violations in both directions on the supplied domain; convex/concave need a global proof plus exclusion of affine overlap.
- No deep input can use a fabricated native certificate or a widened global limit. Rejection/overflow/exhaustion and accepted-population bias remain visible. Source licensing and mathematical admission are distinct gates.

The implemented independent policy is `deep-import-reference-v1`: exact rational scalar parsing, whole-interval sign/range/composition rules, and either a sound strict-composition trace or rigorous opposite-label Jensen evidence. Neither requires two strictly signed midpoint Jensen gaps. Original operand domains remain mandatory even through zero multiplication, constant division and log/exp simplification. Numeric e/E literals explicitly mean base-10 scientific notation in both checker and model instructions. Ambiguous numeric juxtaposition and mismatched grouping reject.

Proof bounds are 4,096-bit exact values, 200,000 point-node evaluations, 33 fixed rational grid points, precision 128/512/2048 bits, exponential arguments within ±4,096 and point binary exponents within ±16,384. Overflow/unresolved strictness yields no admitted label. The importer records exact significant cores of at least 16 nodes/depth 4 plus the full coefficient-blind shape signature; this detects declared relatedness, not full semantic equivalence or training contamination.

Panel publication retains all enumerated source CSV/receipt/candidate/reference files inside its private directory, replays the copy and atomically renames the completed directory. The study fingerprints every non-test API source plus Python import sources/lock and SDK lock/patch. Generation rechecks the pinned tree/environment afterward. Synced immutable receipts precede each send; a synced owner record is atomically linked as the exclusive lock. `unlock` can remove only a demonstrably dead local owner's lock and never resumes paid work. All previously generated exposure, including rejected/quota-filled candidates, is checked before another panel freezes.
