# Module 10 handoff: public comparisons

## Implemented behavior and D-10

D-10 is resolved by the policy in [the implementation plan](../plans/10-leaderboard.md). The public leaderboard projects retained scorer reports; it does not score responses again or populate measured entries with examples.

Eligibility requires published, unexpired, completed execution accounting for all three trials per instance, a complete scored population, no infrastructure failures, stable reported model identity, verified output cap, provider-enforced context handling, and verified native schema acceptance when requested. Routed services also require one stable reported serving identity. Unknown direct OpenAI serving labels remain visibly unknown. Complete invalid, refused and truncated model answers remain included in the scorer denominator and do not independently disqualify the run. Screening populations retain visible scores and explanations but never receive ranks.

Groups bind suite ID and population, generator/depth/verifier/protocol/prompt versions, profile, settings ID, full workload, method, certification backend, depth coverage and concurrency. Requested and reported model/provider/route distinguish entries. The earliest-created eligible currently published execution represents an identical entry; all its current publications remain linked and counted. This limits selective best-run display within available publications, but private, withdrawn and expired history remains unknown. Exact unrounded ties use competition ranks. Display rounding does not affect order; trial min/max is descriptive spread, not a confidence interval or evidence of superiority.

The page fetches current public summaries through a read-only Next route with no credential forwarding, a fixed configured destination, no cache, no redirects, a 20-second deadline, and a 32 MiB response limit. Invalid summaries return an unavailable state, never an empty-list substitute. Listings refresh at least every minute and at known evidence expiry. Selected comparisons fetch at most four fresh public records; withdrawn or expired evidence fails visibly. Anonymous result pages load retained records without private codes and clear displayed results at their known expiry.
