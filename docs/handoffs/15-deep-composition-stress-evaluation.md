## Diagnostic and claim limits

Requested generator depth, realized D/D+1, AST depth and nonlinear path are different measurements. Raw branch counts include every multi-child AST occurrence; simplified branch counts include unique normalized nodes with multiple variable-dependent children. Raw operation counts are occurrences and simplified counts are unique graph nodes. Do not compare their deltas as one common branching/operation measure. Atom-presence counts are unaffected.

Mechanism interactions, derivative scales and range scales are explicitly unestablished/unassessed. Jensen enclosures remain in the individual references. Bounded equality and rigorous pointwise distinctions do not establish full semantic inequivalence, independent functions or training nonexposure. Related groups and unknown pairs remain reported.

Live compatibility is established for the retained requested/reported identities and settings, not for all catalog routes or future availability. A deadline, truncation, unavailable body or unusable response cannot establish a wrong mathematical classification. Single responses do not establish repeat consistency or D-12's stronger repeated-error signal. Preserve all-success and nonmonotonic results.
