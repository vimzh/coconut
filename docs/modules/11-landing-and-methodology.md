## Detailed Work

### The mathematical task

Explain classification as convex, concave, or neither over a stated convex domain. Describe convexity using values along line segments, concavity through the reversed relationship, and neither through evidence that both properties fail under the label policy.

Use verified examples naming each expression, domain, label, and understandable reason. Explain D-01's affine/constant overlap policy instead of suggesting labels are automatically disjoint. Distinguish public illustrations from evaluated instances.

### Difficulty and trusted references

Explain nesting through an approved example of one operation within another. Show depth changing across examples using D-04's actual convention. Describe the suite's depth levels and class coverage without inventing scale or diversity claims.

Describe reference validation and its limits. Uncertainty or failure of a sufficient mathematical check does not establish neither. Explain that models receive the task and domain without reference labels or validation evidence.

### Execution, scoring, and interpretation

Explain the metric report already implemented in module 04 and presented by 09/10, including macro-F1, accuracy, class recall, diagnostic populations, and missing-data limits. No metric or chart integration is deferred to module 13.


Explain D-02 and D-03's reasoning methods, answer behavior, trials, and generation limits. Describe classification of returned answers and treatment of invalid responses, refusals, truncation, and provider errors.

Explain D-05's primary score, overall/by-depth reporting, trial aggregation, and supporting uncertainty measures. Performance on this suite and configuration does not establish general mathematical ability. Describe decided comparison boundaries and eligibility without implying all runs are publicly ranked.

### Visitor journey and factual claims

Explain visitor-owned credentials for OpenAI, OpenRouter, and Hugging Face Inference Providers. Distinguish searchability from hosted execution availability. Explain provider charges and decided credential, interruption, saved-result, and publication behavior when available.

Resolve operational wording against the completed 09 and 10 handoffs and their inherited 05 and 08 policies. Verify working destinations into evaluation and comparisons before completing this module. Do not promise free execution, guaranteed failure, unsupported costs, or planned capabilities.

Keep the paper absent from landing-page text and links; preserve provenance elsewhere. Tie measured numbers and performance claims to project outputs and protocol. Omit unsupported claims.
